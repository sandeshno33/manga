## 🎧 Audio Engineering & Video Editing Quick-Reference

1. **Audio Mixing Levels:**
   - **Voiceovers (Dialogue & Narrator):** `-6 dB` to `-3 dB` (Dominant, clean, crisp high-end).
   - **Sound Effects (SFX):** `-12 dB` (Peaks up to `-6 dB` during explosions and kicks).
   - **Background Music (BGM):** `-18 dB` to `-24 dB` (Ducked automatically by `-6 dB` when voices speak).
2. **Text-to-Speech (TTS) AI Voice Model Mapping:**
   - Paste the exact lines (excluding brackets or including them for tone instructions depending on your AI model) directly into **ElevenLabs**, **CapCut AI Voice**, or **DaVinci Resolve Fairlight**.
3. **Motion Keyframing:**
   - Follow the **Camera Direction** column for exact Ken Burns zoom anchors (e.g., zoom onto eyes, pan across corridors, screen shake on flying kicks).
